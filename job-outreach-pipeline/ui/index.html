Setup checklist

    Run Pipeline

    Run each phase with one click. Live output appears in the console on the right.

    1

    Search for companies

    Semantic web search (Exa) adds new companies to your queue as Pending.

    2

    Scrape contact emails

    Crawls pending companies' sites (Apify) to harvest emails. Runs up to 5 at a time — repeat as needed.

    3

    Generate cover letters

    Builds tailored PDF cover letters for every company with an email. Review them under Letters.

    4

    Send outreach emails

    Sends to up to 15 companies marked Approved, then marks them Sent. A 30–60s delay runs between emails, so this can take a while.
